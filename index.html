The problem we solve

Information asymmetry costs firms 5–10% of value annually.

Digital HR tools speed up admin throughput — but don't resolve the core economic problem: shareholders cannot verify what managers actually do.

MERIDIAN makes that kind of fraud structurally infeasible through permissioned blockchain consensus and immutable audit trails.
